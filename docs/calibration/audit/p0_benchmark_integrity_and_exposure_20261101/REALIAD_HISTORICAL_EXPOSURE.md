## Scope and evidence rule

This ledger uses Git-tracked project protocols, manifests and audit reports only. It does not reopen Real-IAD JSON, image, mask, feature-cache, private-feedback, or untracked output files. “Known” means stated in the tracked record. An aggregate score or category name does not establish exact sample or Source Group membership. Those memberships are marked `UNKNOWN` unless the report expressly binds a frozen tape/digest; this audit does not reconstruct the underlying identities.

## Overall interpretation

Real-IAD outcomes from the original 30-class result and later development-class experiments are already exposed. The four Stage 0.5/0.75/Stage 1-A classes are not independent confirmation data. Switch has additional 512-event R1 engineering exposure. The public all-class catalog and TRAIN census do not certify A/B/C roles or grant G1/G2 permission. Later calibration/R1/R2 records say Mint/PCB test outcomes and masks were not accessed in those phases; however, the original 30-class paper reports all-category aggregate results, so Mint/PCB are not wholly category-level unseen. Historical mask exposure is `UNKNOWN`. An independent confirmation claim therefore requires identity-level, independently attested disjointness evidence; a category name alone cannot establish it.

Exact Source Group intersections across historical runs are `UNKNOWN` unless bound by a listed frozen digest. This audit intentionally does not reopen per-image manifests or infer overlaps from class-level AUROC. R1/G1/G2 authorization and any new Local Correction experiment remain governed by their separate approvals; this report changes none of those statuses.
