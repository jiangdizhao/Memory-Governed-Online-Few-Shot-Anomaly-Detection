# R2-L2D A/B/C Role Adjudication Specification

## H-SOURCE roles

| Role | Purpose | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A | Parameter-design normal reference for later geometry work | Independently reviewed source-group receipt; normal-only population evidence; authorized source manifest. |
| B | Development evaluation after separate stage authorization | Independently reviewed source-group receipt; explicit source/population authorization; evaluation authorization as applicable. |
| C | Sealed independent confirmation after algorithm and hyperparameters are frozen | Independently reviewed source-group receipt; explicit sealed-confirmation authorization and preserved holdout. |

The independence unit is one complete official `official_sample_key`. The existing frozen group digest contract remains unchanged: `sha256:official-sample-group-nul-prefix-utf8.v1`, with SHA-256 over `official-sample-group\0` + the UTF-8 official sample key. The legacy identity spelling `realid-offical-sample-key-v2` is retained. Camera views are not independent assignment units; all views for one physical source group receive the same role because the identity receipt contains one group digest, not view-level assignments.

## Deterministic proposal, not certification

The new identity-only proposer accepts caller-supplied source-group digests, a category, a nonnegative seed, an explicit A/B/C quota, and a policy SHA-256. It ranks each group with the versioned domain-separated hash:

`sha256("sha256:r2-l2d-hsource-group-rank.v1\0" + policy_sha256 + "\0" + category + "\0" + seed + "\0" + source_group_sha256)`

and assigns whole groups in rank order to the requested quotas. Each quota must be positive; quotas must cover the candidate pool exactly. Duplicate/malformed identities, unknown identities, Test metadata, and category-level quota shortages fail closed. The result status is always `PROPOSED`; a policy digest and deterministic ranking do not grant authorization or certify source roles.

No actual L2C private group identities were passed to this proposer. No role quotas or A/B/C memberships were approved or generated by this task. Any future proposal must freeze its quota policy, population, seed, and evidence source before generating membership.

## Partition audit and certification gates

`audit_pairwise_partition` accepts group-level assignments and optionally an authorized candidate universe. It rejects malformed and duplicated IDs, rejects IDs outside the authorized universe, and reports only pairwise counts:

- A∩B
- A∩C
- B∩C

Any nonzero intersection rejects the partition. Zero overlap establishes only the identity-disjointness property; it does not certify provenance, authorization, normality, or scientific independence.

Certification composes the existing L2B `certify_role_identity` contract. For each category and role it requires:

1. A valid role receipt and source-manifest SHA-256 using the frozen identity algorithm.
2. A matching reviewed manifest and independent reviewer attestation.
3. An independently pinned trust-directory digest, a trusted reviewer key, and a source manifest present in the trust directory.
4. For A, independently reviewed normal-only evidence. An empty A reference cannot be certified.
5. For any explicitly empty non-A role, a frozen, verified empty-role policy and reason. A missing receipt never means an empty role.
6. A separately signed and pinned H-SOURCE researcher policy approval. The policy approver cannot also be a role receipt producer or reviewer.
7. Pairwise group disjointness after all three role receipts certify.
8. The same separately authorized category source-manifest SHA across A/B/C and the complete authorized candidate-group universe. Unknown groups and omitted groups reject the partition; a receipt set from different source snapshots cannot be combined.

An Ed25519 signature proves that a key signed a particular payload. It does not, by itself, establish that the signer is independent, that the role allocation is scientifically appropriate, or that the data population is correctly authorized. The reviewer must be separate from the receipt producer, and the trust root must be pinned outside the submitted receipt set. A researcher must separately approve the H-SOURCE policy and permitted stage.
