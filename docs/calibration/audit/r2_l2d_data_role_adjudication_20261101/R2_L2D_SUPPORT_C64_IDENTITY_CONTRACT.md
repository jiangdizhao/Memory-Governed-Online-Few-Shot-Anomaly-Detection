# R2-L2D Support/C64 Identity Contract

## Intended selection

For each category and nonnegative Support Seed, the later protocol must bind:

- Four distinct normal deployment Supports.
- Sixty-four **additional** normal calibration images designated C64.
- No source-group overlap among the four Supports and 64 C64 images.
- All 68 selected identities inside that category's independently certified A normal-reference population.
- A category-specific Fixed Bank identity, category/seed receipt, and any later G1-B M0 scorer/Top-32 receipt.

The 64 C64 images are additional to the four deployment Supports; this is not a strict four-shot-total setting. At least 68 eligible, authorized, pairwise distinct groups are necessary for this selection. The current minimum category pool is 167 groups, which establishes numerical feasibility only. It does not establish the A/B/C split, an approved selection, or a valid bank.

## Existing selection receipt and new composite binding

The existing L2C selection receipt `r2_l2c.support_c64_selection.v1` binds category, support seed, exactly four Support group digests, exactly 64 C64 group digests, producer identity, and receipt SHA-256. The existing L2C verifier enforces digest syntax, candidate-pool inclusion, within-set uniqueness, Support/C64 disjointness, and an independent selection attestation under a pinned reviewer trust directory.

L2D adds the composite schema `r2_l2d.support_c64_fixed_bank_binding.v1` with these fields:

- `category_id`, `support_seed`
- certified A role receipt SHA-256
- Support/C64 selection receipt SHA-256
- fixed-bank digest algorithm and structured fixed-bank SHA-256
- category/seed fixed-support receipt SHA-256
- producer identity and composite receipt SHA-256

The composite record is not sufficient by itself. The validator requires an approved H-SOURCE policy and a separately signed Support/C64 selection authorization. That authorization binds category, seed, the approved policy digest, the certified A receipt, the exact source manifest, and a digest of the complete candidate pool; it explicitly denies Test metadata, outcome-label, and mask access. The validator then requires a certified A receipt, an independently reviewed 4+64 selection, all 68 selected groups inside A, and a separate binding attestation signed by a trusted reviewer. The policy authorizer must differ from the relevant receipt producers and reviewers. The binding reviewer must differ from the binding producer, selection reviewer, A role reviewer, and policy authorizer. The trust directory must match its independently pinned digest. No production signing helper or actual signed authorization/binding is generated by the L2D implementation.

## Fixed-bank identity

The retained historical structured digest algorithm is:

`experiments.calibration.native._fixed_bank_digest.v1:path-shape-dtype-bytes`

The historical digest `8a710d028509350568e976bc3ede66aa59603499cbea525bdd8579322b1a1558` is only for `switch` / seed `0`. `category_fixed_support_receipt_sha256(category, seed, algorithm, bank_sha256)` binds the structured digest to the intended category and seed. The historical switch/seed-0 digest is rejected for any other category or seed, and switch/seed-0 rejects any other digest. It is not a universal bank identity.

G1-B Top-32 work additionally requires the existing L2A category-specific M0 scorer-state and selection receipt, including the correct category's Fixed Bank receipt. A correct Support/C64 binding does not certify a G1-B descriptor source or native score trajectory.

## Selection rule and leakage assertions

Before any selection, a separately reviewed policy must freeze the category, population, seed, deterministic selection procedure, exact 4+64 quotas, and source-manifest identity. The selection may operate only on authorized TRAIN-normal A identities for this contract. The same full source-group digest is the unit for Supports, C64, A/B/C role disjointness, and camera-view grouping. Any duplicate, malformed, unknown, cross-role, Support/C64, category, seed, source-manifest, signature, or trust-root mismatch fails closed.

The current L2C receipts contain no approved Support/C64 memberships. No actual selection or bank binding was created. Synthetic tests use ephemeral test-only cryptographic keys and synthetic group digests; these fixtures are not production signatures or evidence.
