## 3. Fidelity and adaptation boundaries

The 30+12 category scope, new hard cap, `Margin_rho`, K-B/K-M integration, N-shadow, VisA identity/group protocol, P1/P2 manifests, and Static-36+32 split are new protocol/adaptation work. A source file's existence is not evidence of execution, category-general fidelity, data-role validity, or empirical benefit. Preserve unmodified R7/R9/R10/C sources and add any future adapter under a separately authorized final-campaign module with source hashes and tests.

R7/E6 currently checks a five-camera Real-IAD group model, arm-level one-query-per-group, and disjoint Support/C64/TEST group sets. Its event loop updates feedback after sealing and before the next event. It does not provide group-barrier feedback. E6/C is registered for `audiojack`, `regulator`, `switch`, and `rolled_strip_base`, Support Seed 0, Shuffle Seed 0; D2 records the same support seed. R9 has additional four-category Shuffle 11/23 and Support-1 machinery, but that does not establish final all-category, VisA, identity, or C64 parity for P1/P2.

No source in this audit grants campaign authorization. The final reviewed source commit, clean-tree state, data manifests, authorization record, all transitive source fingerprints, and results seals must be separately bound before Gate 1.
