## 3. Fidelity and adaptation boundaries

The 30+12 category scope, new hard cap, `Margin_rho`, K-B/K-M integration, Group-atomic feedback, VisA group-key exception, and any Static-36+32 implementation are new protocol/adaptation work. A source file's existence is not evidence of execution, category-general fidelity, data-role validity, or empirical benefit. Preserve unmodified R7/R9/R10/C sources and add any future adapter under a separately authorized final-campaign module with source hashes and tests.

R7/E6 currently checks a five-camera Real-IAD group model, arm-level one-query-per-group, and disjoint Support/C64/TEST group sets. Its event loop updates feedback after sealing and before the next image. Group-atomic feedback and group-major stream construction are new; exact event-level parity with historical R7/D2/B/C is explicitly not claimed. E6/C is registered for `audiojack`, `regulator`, `switch`, and `rolled_strip_base`, Support Seed 0, Shuffle Seed 0; D2 records the same support seed. R9 has additional four-category Shuffle 11/23 and Support-1 machinery, but that does not establish final all-category, VisA, identity, or C64 parity for P1/P2.

The Gate 0 metadata-only role algorithm ranks official groups and images by deterministic SHA-256 keys, assigns the first four distinct groups to Support and the next 64 to C64, and uses Stream Seed to rank group order. Actual identity hashes and role records are Gate 1 outputs after separate authorization. For VisA, use an official object/group key; if absent, only an explicitly approved duplicate-cluster/image-level exception with limited claims or no online VisA feedback is permitted. No feature inspection of the larger candidate pool is part of the selection.

No source in this audit grants campaign authorization. The final reviewed source commit, clean-tree state, data manifests, authorization record, all transitive source fingerprints, and results seals must be separately bound before Gate 1.
